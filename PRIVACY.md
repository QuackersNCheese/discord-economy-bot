# Privacy Policy for Quackers Ecobot

**Last Updated:** November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Quackers Ecobot** ("the Bot", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you add or interact with the Bot within Discord.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

To provide virtual economy features, leaderboards, and bot commands, Quackers Ecobot collects and stores minimal user and server data:

* **User Data:**
* Discord User ID (unique snowflake identifier).
* In-game economy balance, inventory, level/XP, and command transaction logs.


* **Server (Guild) Data:**
* Discord Server ID and channel IDs where commands or features are enabled.
* Server-specific bot configurations, permissions, and custom prefix settings.


* **We DO NOT Collect:**
* Message contents outside of direct command invocations.
* Personal identifying information (legal names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses).
* Real financial information or payment card details.



### 2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

* Track virtual currency, player stats, items, and leaderboard rankings.
* Execute bot commands and manage server configuration settings.
* Identify bugs, prevent economy exploits, and maintain stability.

We will **never sell, rent, or trade** your data to third parties.

### 3. Data Storage and Security

* All stored IDs and economy values are saved securely in our databases.
* Access to databases and bot tokens is restricted strictly to authorized development team members.

### 4. Data Retention and Deletion Requests

* Data is retained for as long as the Bot remains active or as needed to maintain gameplay continuity.
* **Right to Deletion:** If you wish to have your data permanently deleted from our database:
* Remove the Bot from your server, or
* Contact us via the channels listed below to request manual deletion of your user or server records.



### 5. Third-Party Services

Quackers Ecobot runs on the Discord platform. Use of Discord itself is governed by the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).
